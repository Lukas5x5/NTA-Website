● Now Live — iOS & Android

NTA SkyNav is officially live.

The wait is over: NTA SkyNav is now available on the App Store and Google Play — the navigation app built by balloon pilots, for balloon pilots. GPS navigation, flight recording, live team tracking, weather briefings and load calculation. Everything in one app.

 

Launching with strong partners

Blaser Ballooning — integrated fleet booking directly in the app for Blaser pilots.

capzlog.aero — your flights sync automatically into the certified digital pilot logbook. Navigate. Land. Logged.

 

Your feedback makes this app

This launch is the beginning, not the finish line. We depend on your feedback to improve NTA SkyNav continuously — every report, every idea and every criticism goes directly into development. Use the feedback function in the app or simply reply to this email.

A heartfelt thank you to all beta testers — you shaped this app. Happy landings!
Lukas Reinberger · NTA SkyNav
